# Privacy

Piano & Violin Academy is offline-first and does not require an account. Lesson progress and settings are stored locally on the device. No analytics, ads, or external data uploads are included.

Microphone permission is requested only when the user explicitly opens microphone pitch detection. Audio is processed locally and is not uploaded. Practice recording occurs only after the user taps record and remains on-device. Denying microphone permission does not disable the rest of the app.
